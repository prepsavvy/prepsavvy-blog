# PrepSavvy research and open data

Research data and reproducible figures supporting the [PrepSavvy blog](https://prepsavvy.ai/blog).

## Philosophy research index, 23 October 2026

**Published article:** [Top Philosophy Schools Worldwide for Research and Influence](https://prepsavvy.ai/blog/top-philosophy-schools). This repository contains the source data, calculation methods and code needed to reproduce the research in full. The [article source](articles/top-philosophy-schools.md) includes a direct link back to this reproduction repository.

The release contains 23,457 indexed articles in 31 selected philosophy journals, a research index of 112 institutions with at least 50 articles, and U.S. philosophy-family degree awards for 2018–2024. Research measures institutional affiliations at publication. It does not measure the complete output of current department faculty or all books and languages.

## Reproduce

Python 3.11 or newer is recommended. The calculation and integrity verification use the standard library. No credentials, API key or warehouse account are required.

```sh
python scripts/verify.py
python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
python scripts/graphs.py
python scripts/editorial_analysis.py
python scripts/graphs_v3.py
python scripts/profiles.py
```

The verifier checks every file listed in the source manifest, independently recalculates all 112 school rows, compares numeric values with tolerance 1e-8 and checks the sensitivity results. The graph script writes four figures as both PNG and SVG to `figures/2026-10-23/`. Matplotlib is pinned to 3.10.8; fonts use the bundled, OFL-licensed Manrope family with PrepSavvy’s canonical royal blue.

1. **Research influence:** the top ten by highly cited research contribution.
2. **Research momentum:** all top-twenty institutions, with publication counts in both five-year periods.
3. **Citation concentration:** highly cited share among institutions with at least 100 articles, with Oxford as a comparison.
4. **Influence and growth:** all 112 institutions on a single scatter plot.

The current article uses three revised charts generated by `graphs_v3.py`: influence, highly cited share and publication growth. Bars use brand blue with country flags; growth is green and decline is blue. Every revised chart carries the canonical PrepSavvy wordmark. NYU's journal comparison is retained as supplementary research and is excluded from the article. The original charts remain available for earlier editions.

The article closes with its applicant conclusion. Numbered superscript references link to the CMS source list; the corresponding source metadata is preserved in [top-philosophy-schools.sources.json](articles/top-philosophy-schools.sources.json). Methodology is linked through the research footnote and documented below. Flag assets and their provenance are bundled in [assets/flags](assets/flags/README.md).

The [NYU venue comparison](analysis/2026-10-23/nyu-journals.csv) is derived directly from the frozen article records by `editorial_analysis.py`. Philosophical Studies accounts for 17 of the net 18 fewer NYU-affiliated articles. The [university profiles](profiles/philosophy-universities.json) combine the original research measures with official department sources. Generate the application's serving copy with `python scripts/profiles.py --output ../prepsavvy/public/research/philosophy/universities.json`.

Style tokens and their references are in `assets/chart-style.json`. The bundled font license is in `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`. The original release is preserved at tag `philosophy-2026-10-23`; this editorial revision changes presentation and interpretation, not the frozen ranking data.

## Method and sources

Articles cover publication years 2015–2024. Citations are those indexed at collection on 23 October 2026. The influence score uses citation deciles within each journal and year; boundary ties receive fractional credit, and institutional credit is split equally across eligible education institutions. At least 50 articles are required; fractional citations break score ties. Self-citations are included. Flagged acknowledgment-like affiliation strings receive the documented review rules before institution attribution.

U.S. awards are NCES IPEDS first-major awards in detailed CIP codes within family 38.01, including philosophy, logic, ethics and related philosophy programs. Religion, clinical bioethics and joint degrees classified elsewhere are excluded. Research doctorates use award level 17. Three-year averages require all three reported years. Missing values are blank, not zero. The explicit [crosswalk](data/2026-10-23/crosswalk.json) documents campus aggregation. Rutgers bachelor's awards combine three campuses; doctorates are New Brunswick. Washington degree counts are Seattle only.

Primary sources: [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/), [NCES IPEDS](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/use-the-data). Exact download/API URLs and collection hashes are in the manifest. The OpenAlex extract omits individual author identities and credentialed request parameters. The frozen, curated fields are sufficient to reproduce every published calculation; this is not a complete mirror of the upstream APIs.
