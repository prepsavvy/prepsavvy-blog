# Top Philosophy Schools Worldwide for Research and Influence

Philosophy majors have spent years answering the same question: what are you going to do with that degree?

One answer now comes from the companies building artificial intelligence. Anthropic is advertising a four-month Conceptual Reasoning Fellowship paying $100,000-$167,000. Philosophy is among the disciplines it recruits, and applicants must hold or be completing a relevant PhD, or have equivalent research experience. One proposed assignment is to develop difficult problems that test Claude's judgment in ethics or formal epistemology. [Anthropic fellowship](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5447080008)

Google DeepMind has also advertised a Research Scientist, Philosophy and Human Values role, explicitly welcoming a philosophy PhD or equivalent practical experience. Its work concerns questions about advanced AI, ethics and alignment. [Google's posting](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/139822896062046918-research-scientist-philosophy-and-human-values-deepmind)

Those jobs put a practical purpose behind questions philosophers have debated for centuries. What counts as knowledge? Who is responsible for a decision? Which interests should take priority when they conflict? Companies deploying AI systems have to make choices about those questions in the products people use.

For students considering philosophy, the useful next question is where influential scholarship is happening, and which research communities are building momentum.

PrepSavvy compared 23,457 indexed articles published between 2015 and 2024 in 31 philosophy journals. The comparison covers universities worldwide, with 112 institutions meeting the threshold of 50 articles. Oxford leads the research influence index. UC Irvine is the highest-ranked U.S. institution, followed by NYU.

## The research rankings

Influence measures each university's contribution to papers in the most-cited 10% of their journal and publication year. Citations share credit across the institutions behind a paper. Research growth compares article output in 2015–2019 with 2020–2024.

## Smaller research communities can have a higher concentration of influential work

Oxford leads on the total contribution of highly cited research. UC Irvine leads on its concentration among institutions with at least 100 articles: 21.6% of its attributed output reaches the highly cited group, compared with Oxford's 15.5%.

Geneva and Amsterdam also stand out, at 19.2% and 19.0%. These institutions produced 128 and 132 indexed articles respectively, compared with Oxford's 576. Their smaller publication volume conceals a substantial concentration of work that other researchers cite.

![UC Irvine leads highly cited research concentration at 21.6%, followed by Geneva and Amsterdam.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/prepsavvy/prepsavvy-blog/main/figures/2026-10-23/research-concentration.png)

## Growth is changing the shortlist

Fourteen of the top twenty universities increased their article output. Ruhr University Bochum and Princeton more than doubled theirs; UC Irvine increased its output by 93%. Groningen and Glasgow also grew by more than half.

Oxford expanded from 259 articles to 317 while retaining its lead in influence. NYU and Edinburgh remained near the top of the influence ranking despite publishing fewer articles in the later period. Research influence and expansion describe different strengths.

![Publication counts for all top-20 institutions in 2015–2019 and 2020–2024, with whole-percentage growth.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/prepsavvy/prepsavvy-blog/main/figures/2026-10-23/research-momentum.png)

## The strongest combinations emerge when influence and growth meet

Across all 112 institutions, some of the fastest percentage increases come from universities with relatively small influence scores. Irvine, Princeton and Bochum are more distinctive: each combines a top-twenty influence position with rapid publication growth.

For a student building a research shortlist, Oxford offers the largest contribution of influential work in this comparison. Irvine combines the highest U.S. influence score with a high concentration of highly cited papers and expanding output. Princeton and Bochum show that established research communities can still more than double their publication volume.

![All 112 institutions plotted by research influence and publication growth, highlighting Oxford, UC Irvine, Princeton, Bochum, NYU and Edinburgh.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/prepsavvy/prepsavvy-blog/main/figures/2026-10-23/influence-and-growth.png)

The opportunity for applicants is a broader map of philosophy research. ## How we measured research influence

We analysed 2015–2024 articles in 31 selected English-language philosophy journals using OpenAlex data collected on 23 October 2026. Scores share credit across institutions and citation ties; at least 50 articles are required. The comparison covers institution-affiliated journal research, including earlier faculty appointments. Books and teaching quality are outside its scope. The [public data and code](https://github.com/prepsavvy/prepsavvy-blog) include the journal list, affiliation audit, sensitivity checks and scripts that reproduce every figure.
