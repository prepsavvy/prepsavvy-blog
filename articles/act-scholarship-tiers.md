# ACT Scholarships for 2027: Where the Award Tiers Change

One point on the ACT can move an applicant into a scholarship tier worth thousands of dollars more each year. At the University of Alabama, the published annual award rises from **$15,000 at 29 to $24,000 at 30**, holding the GPA requirement constant.[¹](#source-1)

That gives students a reason to look at funding before deciding what score to aim for. A university's scholarship table can make a particular threshold more useful than a general ambition to score “a little higher.”

We compared published merit awards at three Alabama public universities for fall 2027 entry. The examples below show where the tiers change, what the score means at each institution and which conditions students need to check.

## Three thresholds worth knowing

Each comparison keeps the university's published GPA condition constant. These are annual cash awards; additional housing support appears separately.

| University | Published GPA condition | ACT threshold | Annual cash award | Annual increase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alabama[¹](#source-1) | 3.50+ | 29 → 30 | $15,000 → $24,000 | **$9,000** |
| UAB[²](#source-2) | 3.50–3.99 | 31 → 32 | $17,500 → $22,500 | **$5,000** |
| North Alabama[³](#source-3) | Unweighted 3.51–4.00 | 29 → 30 | $8,500 → $10,000 | **$1,500** |

At North Alabama, the higher Presidential tier also includes conditional residence-hall funding of **$4,000 annually for two years**. Apartments are excluded. That benefit belongs alongside the cash award, with its own conditions and duration.[³](#source-3)

The table is a comparison of published thresholds. Each university has its own residency, citizenship and GPA rules, so it does not establish what the same applicant would receive at all three.

## The award increases arrive in steps

Alabama's grid illustrates why the next threshold matters. For its 3.50+ GPA category, the annual cash award is $10,000 at 28, $15,000 at 29 and $24,000 at 30. Moving from 30 to 31 leaves that award unchanged; reaching 32 moves it to $28,000.[¹](#source-1)

## Check which score the university uses

Alabama requires an official ACT composite from one test date for automatic scholarships. UAB also excludes superscores. North Alabama uses an ACT superscore calculated from the best English, Reading and Math sections.[¹](#source-1)[²](#source-2)[³](#source-3)

Funding can also exist without a submitted score. UAB lists a test-optional scholarship range of $7,500–$28,500, with selection based on the application; the upper amount is not an automatic award. North Alabama lists no-ACT tiers. Alabama keeps competitive scholarships open to applicants without scores, separately from its test-dependent automatic awards.[¹](#source-1)[²](#source-2)[³](#source-3)

Timing matters too. Alabama lists December 4 for Summer/Fall 2027 applications and official scores. UAB lists May 1 for admission applications, with final eligible ACT and SAT test windows in February and March. North Alabama has a March 1, 2027 priority deadline and accepts official scores through the April 2027 test date.[¹](#source-1)[²](#source-2)[³](#source-3)

## Set a target that matches your application

Before planning another test, identify the award tier, confirm how your GPA is calculated and check your residency or citizenship eligibility. North Alabama's freshman scholarship rules, for example, limit eligibility to U.S. citizens.[³](#source-3)

Then check renewal. Alabama requires a cumulative UA GPA of at least 3.0 and at least 67% of attempted UA credits earned. UAB's current grid does not establish a four-year funding promise, so annual figures should stay annual.[²](#source-2)[⁵](#source-5)
